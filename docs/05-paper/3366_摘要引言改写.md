# Revised Abstract

DCCA-GS is built on the HAC++ anchor-hash entropy-coding framework and keeps the HAC++ main pipeline (structured anchors, hash-grid context, conditional entropy model, arithmetic coding, G-PCC geometry), adding five evaluated components:

1. decoder-reproducible content-adaptive formula quantization, in which every quantization step is recomputed on both ends from decoder-reconstructable structural quantities, so no per-anchor step data is transmitted;
2. render-sensitivity supervision, a training-only mechanism that steers the complexity network without changing the bitstream;
3. mixed-precision post-training quantization of the decoder MLPs, with a per-group bit-width assignment derived from measured sensitivity to rendering quality and entropy modeling, charged to the reported size;
4. training-side anchor sparsity with ADMM projection, which imposes a hard anchor budget during training and re-adapts the surviving anchors under that budget;
5. Mini-Splatting depth re-initialization, a zero-side-information placement mechanism that re-seeds anchors from visible scene depth while keeping the SPA budget pinned: +0.199 dB PSNR at +2.5% payload on playroom, but −0.012 dB on 4-28 110k and −0.073 dB on drjohnson 30k.

Components (1) and (2) are jointly designed, and their interaction is explicitly analyzed in the ablation study. On the 4-28 UAV scene, the 110k-iteration operating point with MLP quantization reaches 28.82 dB PSNR at 5.49 MiB decode-required size, versus 28.31 dB at 6.95 MiB for the paper-reported HAC++ point on the same scene; on Deep Blending, playroom reaches 30.73 dB at 4.22 MiB and drjohnson 30.04 dB at 6.85 MiB. Quantitative analyses: codec efficiency (actual/estimated bits close to 1.0), a KL-redundancy lower bound for the scaling field, bitstream composition, rate-distortion behavior over lambda and training steps, and documented negative results (context conditioning below 2.3%, residual coding, sensitivity side information, and scene-dependent MiniSplat gains). All bitstreams pass bit-exact standalone decoding verification.

# Revised Introduction (contributions part)

1. **Decoder-reproducible content-adaptive formula quantization.** Quantization steps are generated on the fly from decoder-reconstructable structural quantities — local anchor density, predicted scaling anisotropy, predicted offset energy, and active mask ratio. The image-side statistics used during development are excluded from the formula mode and are therefore neither transmitted nor required at decode time; the bitstream carries no per-anchor step array. Training uses a straight-through estimator so that the quantization path is shared by training, encoding, and decoding.

2. **Render-sensitivity supervision.** The complexity network is trained to match a bounded, exponentially smoothed target derived from render-loss gradient norms. Training-only, zero side information. The high-rate gain is modest (about 0.1 dB); in the low-rate SPA regime, removing it reduces PSNR by 0.42 dB (see Section 3.4).

3. **Mixed-precision MLP weight quantization.** Per-output-channel symmetric post-training quantization with static arithmetic coding. The bit-width assignment is not uniform: the complexity and deform networks are quantized to 8 bits, while the opacity, covariance, color, and grid networks remain at 16 bits. Derived from per-MLP sensitivity ablation: 8-bit opacity directly degrades rendering; 8-bit grid degrades the entropy model and increases attribute rate; complexity/deform tolerate 8 bits with negligible loss.

4. **Training-side anchor sparsity (SPA).** Unlike encode-side top-k pruning, SPA imposes a hard anchor budget during training via ADMM projection and a linear budget ramp anchored to the historical maximum anchor count, so surviving anchors re-adapt under the budget. At equal anchor count it outperforms encode-side top-k pruning by 5.5 dB; the resulting operating point trades 0.84 dB PSNR for a 74% size reduction relative to the non-SPA model (see Section 3.4).

5. **Mini-Splatting depth re-initialization.** After anchor growth stops, visible scene depth is re-projected and the resulting surface points refresh anchors while the ADMM budget remains pinned at the pre-densification count: placement change without adding side information. On Deep-Blending playroom (30k, λ=0.004, SPA 0.85), PSNR improves from 30.221 to 30.420 dB at +2.5% payload; over the SPA ratio curve, BD-PSNR +0.124 dB and BD-rate −8.6%. The gain is not reproduced on 4-28 110k (SPA+Mini 28.736 dB / 5.449 MiB vs SPA baseline 28.748 dB / 5.514 MiB), and drjohnson 30k is slightly negative (29.315 vs 29.390 dB at −1.8% payload). Reported as scene-dependent; no universal-improvement claim.

In the implementation and experiments, components (1) and (2) are referred to as I2 and I6; they are jointly designed, and their interaction is analyzed in the ablation study. All experiments follow a strict bitstream contract with standalone decoding verification; this constitutes the evaluation protocol of this work rather than a separate technical contribution.
